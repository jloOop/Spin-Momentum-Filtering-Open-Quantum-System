# Project scope and evidence boundaries

This repository supports a finite-model study of environmental susceptibility in a spin–momentum absorbing-boundary detector. It is the open-system extension of the related detector-only work. The roof is the sole monitored sink; transverse relaxation, excitation and number dephasing are trace preserving and unobserved. Numerical representation loss is recorded separately from detector clicks.

## Four different reproducibility levels

| Level | Support here |
|---|---|
| Inspect supplied evidence | Compact records, generating-source identities, historical audits and the release manifest. |
| Reconstruct observables from saved arrays | Tested CPU commands in REPRODUCING.md; exact scope is stated for each group. |
| Rerun original simulations | Selected historical source is supplied, but complete runtime/reference closure and production execution are not claimed. |
| Restart historical states | Not supported: endpoint histories and checkpoint manifests do not replace missing state factors, sector states and policy anchors. |

New release-integrity and reduction checks establish only their stated software/data properties. Historical PASS, QUALIFIED PASS, FAIL and scientific_acceptance=false fields remain historical evidence. They are never replaced by a global scientific acceptance flag.

## What the scientific comparisons establish

**Scalar and spinor controls.** Under factorization and a fixed longitudinal marginal, trace-preserving transverse dynamics cannot change the complete roof-integrated scalar law. The selected finite-step factorized controls test this structure. Monolithic scalar discretizations can violate the desired factorization at finite step; the matched artifact and timestep controls remain included. The boundary operator K changes the surviving spinor state rather than adding loss. No experimental realization, universal confinement scaling or unrestricted continuum spinor-plus-bath theorem follows from these finite calculations.

**Canonical response.** The selected 18 roles retain the canonical hashes and the distinction between original local failures and later independent aggregate interpretation. Task08 uses strict and operational uncertainty policies separately. Its inherited 2.354% representation and 0.4% longitudinal allowances are not newly measured continuum error bars, and historical rerun coverage is incomplete. The root D1 and P2-I identities are the 4800 and 4807 records, not the earlier look-alike alternatives. Detector step, source insertion spacing and response-panel width are distinct. Jump and anticommutator contributions are combined using the recorded causal convention, retaining censoring and separate roof/survival residuals.

**Preparation, confinement and horizon.** The broader sampled confinement family is descriptive, not a matched convergence campaign at every frequency. The omega=144 insertion/horizon records support opposite preparation signs and exact affine mixture cancellation of a terminal derivative with nonzero timing response. Threshold digits identify arithmetic on saved curves, not uncertainty to that precision. Excited-state detector-step, shell and longitudinal refinements remain incomplete. The extension command reduces the archived derived response arrays and checks supplied tables; the three native T20 kernels, their matched detector-only baselines and the full historical kernel reconstruction are not bundled.

**Finite transverse operations.** The operation study uses a specific trace-preserving resolvent–Kraus channel, not a simulated continuous thermal bath pulse. Opposite changes occur at a common final deadline; both tested effects are negative at equal elapsed time after intervention. The early action-halving curvature check was reported resolved; the late remainder remains below its declared numerical resolution indicator. These observations do not demonstrate bath non-Markovianity.

**Second shell.** Detector-only propagation tests a frozen launch-kernel prediction and distinguishes equal-energy, equal-initial-RMS-momentum preparations. Output-shell partitions do not identify a unique microscopic return sequence. Only the selected endpoint evidence is bundled; reproducing the full second-shell figures requires omitted reconstructed arrays and native propagation outputs.

**Initial-spin null.** V10 describes a distinct reported null for parity-invariant transverse preparations, reflection–spin covariant finite dynamics, and a roof-integrated, spin-unresolved measurement. It does not imply absence of spin–motion coupling or insensitivity for spin-resolved, position-resolved or parity-breaking protocols. The full spin-angle campaign is not newly reconstructed here.

**Finite rates.** The [Task09 results page](data/finite_rate/README.md) separates the historical poster support (5.65, 8.3, 3.6) from later saved support (6, 10, 3.825) for π/3200, π/1600 and π/800. All 27 arrays per historical branch were verified as exact prefixes of the bundled longer histories, including paired zeros and time grids. Those inherited prefixes are not independent runs. Native probability differences show prompt suppression, later sign structure and negative launch-cumulative changes at all saved endpoints; a positive late window alone does not establish net enhancement. The poster’s printed 0.9992 ratio and 0.072% deviation are preserved as historical statements, with the unrounded reproduction and rounding discrepancy documented. The results page gives the shared [numerical-policy qualifications](data/finite_rate/README.md#numerical-policy-and-evidence-scope); descriptive rate scaling does not establish complete finite-rate convergence.
